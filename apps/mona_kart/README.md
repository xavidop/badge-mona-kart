# Mona Kart

A pseudo-3D kart race against five rival Octocats: three laps, coffee boosts, bugs to dodge, and a best lap the badge remembers.

Steer by tilting the badge or with Left and Right. The kart accelerates by
itself; Back brakes. Grab a coffee for a burst of speed, avoid the bugs, and
bump past the rivals on the way to the flag. Menu on the title screen switches
between tilt and button steering.

## Controls

* Tilt, or Left / Right: steer
* Select: start the race; race again from the results
* Back: brake; return to the title from the results
* Menu: switch between tilt and button steering (title screen)

## Requirements

No network. The best lap, best race and win count are saved under `/state`.
The sprite sheets in `assets/` are drawn by the repository's `tools/build_sprites.py`.
